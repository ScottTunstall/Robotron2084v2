## Round 11 (`9968717`) — what changed

1. **Grunts now stagger** (notes §29). R5 `$39CD/$39E6`: body every 4 vblanks
   (`LDA #$04`); move countdown re-rolled `RND(1..ROBSPD)` (`$D042` = A×RND with
   INCA, **never 0**) on each step → uniform random 1..ROBSPD bodies between
   steps. Step = 4 arcade px (8 screen px) per axis past a 2-arcade-px (4
   screen px) dead zone, axes independent (diagonals). RWDP frame advances per
   BODY, not per step — a MISREAD, see §30/priority item 1 below (corrected to
   per-step the same evening). `WaveTable.GruntMoveDelay` (already the ROM $BE5C values:
   20,15,15…) is now consumed as the re-roll LIMIT. Port was fixed-period
   `PortTicks(ROBSPD)` with 2-px steps — 2× step rate, zero variance.
2. **"Screen full of sparks" = spheroid drop rate, NOT enforcer fire rate.**
   Enforcer fire verified ROM-exact and untouched: 3-vblank AI pass,
   `RND(1..ENSTIM)` re-arm BEFORE the 20-spark cap ($1408–1414), 8-enforcer cap.
   Real bug: the spheroid drop countdown ($0009,U) decrements once per full
   8-frame animation cycle — the 2-vblank body ($121A–121F) only reaches the
   DEC at $11F2 on the last-frame pass ($11EC–11F0) = **16 vblanks/step**. Port
   ran 4 vblanks/step (round-7 "1 step per 4 ROM ticks" miscount) → 8-enforcer
   cap filled in ~2–4 s (arcade ~10–25 s) → the arcade's late-wave spark storm
   started immediately. Fix: `Spheroid.CountdownToTicks = PortTicks(steps×16)`.
